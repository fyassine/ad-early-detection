  \caption{Subject-level functional connectivity graph construction pipeline
    (illustrated, preview). (1) Resting-state four-dimensional \ac{BOLD} \ac{fMRI}
    scans are parcellated into $|V| = 200$ cortical \acp{ROI} using the Schaefer-200
    atlas. (2) Mean BOLD time series ($\mathbf{T} \in \mathbb{R}^{200 \times L}$) are
    extracted per region. (3) Pairwise Pearson correlation followed by Fisher
    $z$-transformation yields the continuous functional connectivity matrix
    $C \in \mathbb{R}^{200 \times 200}$. (4a) Raw connectivity rows form 200-dimensional
    node feature vectors ($X = C$). (4b) Symmetrised $k$-nearest-neighbour
    sparsification over $|C|$ constructs the binary adjacency matrix $A$. (5) The
    resulting visit graph $\mathcal{G}_i^{(t)}$ is paired with normalized interval
    timing $[\Delta t_t]$ across the longitudinal sequence. Inset panels (brain
    slices, connectivity heatmaps, node/edge diagrams) are synthetic schematic
    illustrations of the construction steps, not measured subject data.}
  \label{fig:graph-construction-pipeline-preview}
\end{figure}
