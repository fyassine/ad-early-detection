# Challenges

## Label definition

Converter vs. stable MCI depends on the follow-up window. The window choice trades
cohort size against label noise.

## Irregular longitudinal sampling

Visits occur at uneven intervals with missing follow-ups, producing variable-length
sequences. This forces masking/padding or time-aware modeling choices.

Longitudinal evaluation protocols are not standardised.

## Site and protocol heterogeneity

ADNI phases differ in field strength and acquisition protocol, which are specific to
multi-site fMRI, compared to DELCODE, where data is homogeneous.
